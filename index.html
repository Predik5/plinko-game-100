How the Plinko board works

The Plinko board is a triangle of pegs. Each time the ball hits a peg, it can move left or right, and that small decision repeats until the ball reaches the bottom. The more rows you add, the more times the ball changes direction, so the pockets become more spread out and the result pattern becomes sharper.

Payouts, odds, and house margin
